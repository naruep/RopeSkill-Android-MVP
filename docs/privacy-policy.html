RopeSkill Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

RopeSkill processes camera frames and body-pose landmarks on your Android device and does not retain or send them. If you explicitly choose RECORD & START, RopeSkill saves a video of the visible app screen on your device. RopeSkill does not automatically upload or share that recording.

Who we are

RopeSkill is published by Naruep Jukping. For privacy questions, contact naruep.j@gmail.com.

Camera and on-device pose processing

RopeSkill requests camera permission when you start Training. The live camera feed is used to show your preview and estimate body pose so the app can count Basic Bounce jump-rope movements. Camera frames and pose landmarks are processed in memory on the device. Outside the optional screen-recording feature described below, RopeSkill does not retain camera frames or pose landmarks.

Optional screen recording

Before Speed 30, you can choose RECORD & START or start without recording. Android asks for screen-capture consent for every recording session and lets you choose what to share. When recording is enabled, RopeSkill saves the visible RopeSkill screen, including the camera preview and on-screen training information, as an MP4 video in Movies/RopeSkill. The recording does not capture the microphone or internal audio.

Recordings stay on your device. RopeSkill does not upload them. A video is shared with another app only if you tap SHARE VIDEO and select a destination in Android's share sheet. Screen recording stops when the session finishes, you leave RopeSkill, or Android ends the screen-capture session.

Information stored on your device

RopeSkill stores the following information only inside the app on your device:

  • an optional nickname;
  • training history, including exercise type, jump count, duration, and session start and completion times;
  • preferences such as sound, vibration, countdown, units, theme, music enabled state, and music volume; and
  • the display name and Android document URI for an audio file that you explicitly select, together with read access granted by Android.

RopeSkill uses this information only to provide its settings, training, results, and history features. The selected audio file is played from its original storage location and is not copied into RopeSkill.

Data collection, sharing, and third parties

RopeSkill has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud sync, or Internet permission. It does not transmit user data to Naruep Jukping or third parties. The app uses AndroidX, CameraX, MediaPipe, Media3, DataStore, and Room libraries locally to provide its features; these components are not configured by RopeSkill to transmit user data.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only while the Training camera experience is active, for live preview and on-device pose estimation.
  • Vibration: used for local training feedback.
  • Selected audio access: granted through Android's document picker only for a music file you choose.
  • Screen capture: Android MediaProjection consent is used only after you choose RECORD & START. A foreground-service notification remains visible while recording.

Retention, deletion, and backup

Local settings and training history remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall RopeSkill. You can delete individual sessions from History, remove selected-music access or reset preferences from Settings. Optional screen recordings are stored in the shared Movies/RopeSkill collection and can be deleted in Gallery or Files. Uninstalling RopeSkill removes app-private data but may not remove videos previously saved to Movies/RopeSkill.

RopeSkill disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its app data. Because RopeSkill has no accounts or servers, there is no server-held account or user data to delete.

Security

Local settings and history are kept in RopeSkill's private Android app storage. Optional recordings are kept in Android's shared media storage. Android permission controls protect camera and selected-document access. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, but RopeSkill minimizes risk by keeping processing and retained information on the device and by disabling app-data backup and transfer.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before RopeSkill changes how it accesses, stores, collects, or shares data. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
